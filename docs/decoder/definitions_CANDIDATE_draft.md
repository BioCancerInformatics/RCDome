## 1. Apoptosis (intrinsic)
- **Category:** Subtype of Apoptosis
- **Year of Discovery:** 2002 (first "intrinsic apoptosis" in title)
- **Operational Definition:** The mitochondrial (Bcl-2-regulated) apoptotic pathway, initiated by mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilization and blocked by antiapoptotic Bcl-2 family proteins (Bcl-2, Bcl-xL); contrasted with the death-receptor (extrinsic) pathway (Chen et al., 2002).
- **Key Molecules:** Bcl-2, Bcl-xL, Bax.
- **Inflammatory?:** No
- **Biological Relevance:** the dominant apoptotic route for intracellular stress and developmental death.
- **Overlaps:** subtype of Apoptosis; executes via caspases; converges with extrinsic pathway.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 6 / 7 / 7 / 4 / 8 / 7 → **S = 6.40**
- **References:** Chen et al. 2002, PMID 12186923 (CANDIDATE — verify as first "intrinsic apoptosis" use).

## 2. Apoptosis (extrinsic)
- **Category:** Subtype of Apoptosis
- **Year of Discovery:** 2004 (first "extrinsic apoptosis" in title)
- **Operational Definition:** The death-receptor apoptotic pathway, initiated through CD95/Fas receptor oligomerization, FADD recruitment, and caspase-8 activation (Chatfield & Eastman, 2004).
- **Key Molecules:** CD95/Fas, FADD, caspase-8.
- **Inflammatory?:** No
- **Biological Relevance:** immune-mediated and ligand-induced apoptosis.
- **Overlaps:** subtype of Apoptosis; crosses to intrinsic pathway via Bid.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 6 / 7 / 6 / 4 / 7 / 7 → **S = 6.10**
- **References:** Chatfield & Eastman 2004, PMID 15451440 (CANDIDATE — verify).

## 3. Autophagy-dependent cell death
- **Category:** Core pending RCD form
- **Year of Discovery:** 2009 (term in title; concept earlier)
- **Operational Definition:** Cell death executed through autophagy, characterized by accumulation of acidic and double-membrane vesicles, phosphatidylserine exposure and zeiosis, in which inhibition of autophagosome formation prevents death (Mora et al., 2009).
- **Key Molecules:** autophagy machinery (autophagosome formation).
- **Inflammatory?:** not specified
- **Biological Relevance:** a death route for apoptosis-resistant cells; basal autophagic-flux blockade.
- **Overlaps:** autophagy; "autophagic cell death" concept predates (Schweichel & Merker 1973).
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 5 / 5 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 4 → **S = 4.85**
- **References:** Mora et al. 2009, PMID 18942750 (CANDIDATE — verify; earlier "autophagic cell death" literature exists).

## 4. Regulated Necrosis
- **Category:** Umbrella (parent term)
- **Year of Discovery:** 2011
- **Operational Definition:** Umbrella term for genetically controlled, non-apoptotic necrotic cell death; the founding usage concerns RIP3-driven regulated necrosis negatively controlled by FADD (Galluzzi, Kepp & Kroemer, 2011).
- **Key Molecules:** RIP3, FADD.
- **Inflammatory?:** Yes (necrosis)
- **Biological Relevance:** the regulated-necrosis superfamily (necroptosis, pyroptosis, MPT-driven necrosis, …).
- **Overlaps:** parent of necroptosis, pyroptosis, MPT-driven necrosis.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 6 / 4 / 6 / 5 / 7 / 6 → **S = 5.45**
- **References:** Galluzzi et al. 2011, PMID 21894190 (CANDIDATE — verify; concept earlier via necroptosis, Degterev 2005).

## 5. MPT-driven necrosis
- **Category:** Core pending RCD form
- **Year of Discovery:** 2016 (term in title; concept earlier)
- **Operational Definition:** A regulated necrosis initiated by abrupt loss of osmotic homeostasis of the inner mitochondrial membrane through the permeability transition pore complex (PTPC), implicated in acute post-mitotic cell loss in cardiac and cerebral ischemia (Izzo et al., 2016).
- **Key Molecules:** permeability transition pore complex (PTPC).
- **Inflammatory?:** Yes
- **Biological Relevance:** mitochondrial-driven necrosis in ischemia-reperfusion injury.
- **Overlaps:** downstream of Mitochondrial permeability transition (MPT, Trigger); overlaps necrosis.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 5 / 5 / 5 / 4 / 5 / 5 → **S = 4.85**
- **References:** Izzo et al. 2016, PMID 27161573 (CANDIDATE — review; term coining ~2008 needs verification).

## 6. Lysosomal cell death
- **Category:** Synonym of Lysosome-dependent cell death
- **Year of Discovery:** 2004 (term in title; concept de Duve 1959)
- **Operational Definition:** Cell death mediated by the cysteine-cathepsin lysosomal pathway (cathepsin B/L), which sensitizes immortalized and transformed cells to TNF cytotoxicity (Fehrenbacher et al., 2004). Synonym of Lysosome-dependent cell death (already defined).
- **Key Molecules:** cathepsin B, cathepsin L.
- **Inflammatory?:** Yes (lysosomal rupture)
- **Biological Relevance:** lysosomal protease-dependent death; tumor-cell sensitization.
- **Overlaps:** synonym of Lysosome-dependent cell death; overlaps autophagy/apoptosis.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 4 / 5 / 4 / 4 / 5 / 4 → **S = 4.35**
- **References:** Fehrenbacher et al. 2004, PMID 15289336 (CANDIDATE — verify; concept earlier, de Duve 1959).

## 7. Metalloptosis
- **Category:** Umbrella (metal-ion RCD)
- **Year of Discovery:** 2025 (term coined)
- **Operational Definition:** Umbrella term for metal-ion-based regulated cell death controlled by one or more signaling cascades, encompassing ferroptosis, cuproptosis, lysozincrosis, manganism, calcicoptosis, and NECSO (Wang et al., 2025).
- **Key Molecules:** metal ions (iron, copper, zinc, manganese, calcium, sodium).
- **Inflammatory?:** member-dependent
- **Biological Relevance:** metal-homeostasis-driven death; nanomedicine targeting.
- **Overlaps:** umbrella of ferroptosis, cuproptosis, lysozincrosis, NECSO, etc.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 4 / 4 / 4 / 6 / 5 / 5 → **S = 4.45**
- **References:** Wang et al. 2025, PMID 41094656 (CANDIDATE — review that coins the umbrella; verify).

## 9. NECSO (necrosis by sodium overload)
- **Category:** Legacy / unresolved (provisional)
- **Year of Discovery:** 2025
- **Operational Definition:** Necrotic cell death caused by sodium overload, induced by the compound necrocide 1 (NC1) through persistent activation of the TRPM4 cation channel, promoting Na⁺ influx and necrosis (Fu et al., 2025).
- **Key Molecules:** TRPM4, Na⁺, necrocide 1 (NC1).
- **Inflammatory?:** Yes (necrosis)
- **Biological Relevance:** regulated Na⁺-influx necrosis; cardiac-arrhythmia TRPM4 mutants show increased vulnerability.
- **Overlaps:** metalloptosis umbrella (sodium); overlaps necrosis.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 3 / 4 / 3 / 6 / 4 / 3 → **S = 3.80**
- **References:** Fu et al. 2025, PMID 39915626 (CANDIDATE — verify). *Committee promotion from legacy status required.*

## 10. Vacuolar cell death
- **Category:** Morphology / contextual
- **Year of Discovery:** 2004
- **Operational Definition:** Cell death characterized by conspicuous cytoplasmic vacuolization (autophagosomes, autophagic bodies and/or vacuoles); autophagy-gene (atg1) disruption in Dictyostelium revealed that a hidden non-vacuolar death pathway can still operate when vacuolization is blocked (Kosta et al., 2004).
- **Key Molecules:** atg1 (autophagy machinery).
- **Inflammatory?:** not specified
- **Biological Relevance:** developmental vacuolar death; conservation across kingdoms.
- **Overlaps:** overlaps autophagic cell death; morphology overlapping paraptosis/methuosis.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 3 / 3 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 3 → **S = 3.25**
- **References:** Kosta et al. 2004, PMID 15358773 (CANDIDATE — verify).

## 11. Eryptosis
- **Category:** Cell-type-specific program (erythrocytes)
- **Year of Discovery:** 2006 (term in title; coined 2001)
- **Operational Definition:** Suicidal death of erythrocytes characterized by cell shrinkage, membrane blebbing, protease (calpain) activation, and phosphatidylserine exposure, triggered by oxidative stress via prostaglandin E₂/Ca²⁺-channel and phospholipase A₂/ceramide pathways (Lang et al., 2006).
- **Key Molecules:** Ca²⁺-permeable channels, ceramide, calpain, erythropoietin (inhibitor).
- **Inflammatory?:** No (phosphatidylserine-mediated clearance)
- **Biological Relevance:** turnover of defective erythrocytes; escapes hemolysis; excessive eryptosis → anemia.
- **Overlaps:** shares PS-exposure clearance with apoptosis/efferocytosis.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 5 / 5 → **S = 4.15**
- **References:** Lang et al. 2006, PMID 16910766 (CANDIDATE — verify; term coined ~2001, Lang).

## 13. Hypersensitive response
- **Category:** Taxon-specific program (plants)
- **Year of Discovery:** pre-PubMed (Stakman 1915; earliest PubMed-indexed 1969)
- **Operational Definition:** Rapid, localized programmed cell death of plant cells at the site of pathogen infection that restricts pathogen spread; the founding concept predates PubMed (Stakman 1915), with the earliest indexed plant study on chloroplast alteration during the hypersensitive reaction to TMV (1969). *Prose requires committee primary-source citation before locking.*
- **Key Molecules:** not specified (to source from plant HR literature)
- **Inflammatory?:** No (localized, containment)
- **Biological Relevance:** plant innate immunity and pathogen containment.
- **Overlaps:** taxon-specific RCD; parallels animal innate-immunity death.
- **C1–C6 (DRAFT):** 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 5 / 3 → **S = 3.90**
- **References:** earliest PubMed PMID 4977772 (1969); founding = Stakman 1915 / Klement 1963–1967 (committee to supply exact citation).
