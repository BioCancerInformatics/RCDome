RCDomeThe dome is the visual signature: its cupola rim carries the 53 canonicalized RCD-related entities as nodes colored by their adjudicated ontological level — L1 modalities (19, gold), L2 subtypes (7, blue), L3 triggers (2, green), L4 Functional/contextual (13, purple), and L5 Generic/umbrella/taxon (12, orange) — over a constellation of the 9,265 HGNC-validated genes. Only 19 of the 53 entities are bona fide RCD modalities; the rest are subtypes, triggers, contextual programs, or umbrella concepts, and the level color encodes that distinction.
